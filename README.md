I am a Healthcare IT professional who recently graduated with my B.S. of Cybersecurity and Information Assurance. I recently completed a project working a small acquisition team, where I converted the IT infrastructure a rural hospital to new standards after we were acquired by a larger healthcare nonprofit. My work has been traditionally hands on, working with every system in the hospital. However now that my project successfully wrapped, I have been transferred to remote troubleshooting work as a Service Desk Technician. I am now working in a much larger environment, providing support to over 35,000 end users. I believe this acquisition has been and will continue to be a thrilling opportunity and proving ground. I already was able to lead desktop level projects, and regularly worked with department leads, and represent IT in multi department initiatives. My career is now firmly rooted in healthcare IT. I aspire to build and secure seamless IT systems that ensure quality patient care and protects patient's medical data. 

As a student at WGU I was a founding member of our WiCyS Chapter, later I was elected to be president of the chapter for '25/'26. I was a WiCyS national scholarship award winner in 2026. I am passionate about continuous learning, problem solving, and building a safer cyber landscape. 

## Objective

I am a believer in constant growth, and always keeping an eye open for opportunities for professional growth in the Healthcare IT sector. Within Healthcare IT, I am particularity drawn to Cloud / Security work.
